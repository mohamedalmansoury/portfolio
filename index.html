Selected Projects

Nile Floodplain SAR Flood Mapping
Graduation Project · 2026

End-to-end automated flood mapping web app built on Sentinel-1 SAR data via Google Earth Engine, deployed as an interactive Streamlit tool. Refined Lee speckle filtering and Otsu thresholding distinguish water from land; SRTM elevation data masks high-slope terrain (>5°) to cut false positives. Supports custom ROI upload (GeoJSON/Shapefile), real-time flood-area statistics, and GeoTIFF/Shapefile export to Google Drive.
